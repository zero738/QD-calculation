# Active and legacy models

- `B0`, `H1`, `H2` are the active complete-repeat thickness models generated by the current scripts.
- `T0`, `T1`, `T2` are the first PR revision. They sliced the Cu2Te cell into half sheets, causing different top terminations. They are retained only for traceability and are not regenerated or used by current tests.

Do not mix T-series and B/H-series energies or thickness definitions.
